How to Use the AI Fish Classifier

Follow these simple steps to upload your fish image and receive an AI-powered species prediction.

1

Upload Your Image

Upload a clear image of the fish you want to identify.

Supported formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG

For the best results:

  • Make sure the fish is clearly visible.
  • Use a well-lit, focused image.
  • Avoid heavily blurred or obstructed images.
  • Keep the fish as the main subject of the image.
2

Let the AI Analyse the Image

Once your image is uploaded, the AI model will analyse its visual features and compare them with patterns learned from the Fish-Pak dataset.

Vision Transformer (ViT)

The system uses a Vision Transformer (ViT) model trained specifically for fish species classification.

3

View Your Results

After processing, the system will display the following information:

Predicted Species

The fish species identified by the model.

Probability

The model's confidence distribution across the possible species classes.

A higher probability indicates that the model considers that class more likely, but it does not guarantee that the prediction is correct.

Important Notes

The classifier is designed for the fish species represented in its training dataset.

  • Poor lighting, blurry images, unusual angles, or unfamiliar fish may affect the prediction.
  • AI predictions should be independently verified when accurate species identification is important.
